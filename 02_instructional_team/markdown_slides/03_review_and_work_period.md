# Developing your Project Proposal

* Add your project plan to your team's README file. This plan should include details on:

    * What value does your project bring to the industry?
    * How will you answer your business question with your chosen dataset?
    * What are the risks and uncertainties?
    * What methods and technologies will you use?

* Although these details may change as you continue your project, by now you should know most of these details with a relatively high degree of confidence.
